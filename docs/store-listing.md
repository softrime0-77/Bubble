## English (en-US)

**App name** (max 30)
Bubble: Cats vs Hamsters

**Short description** (max 80)
Cats vs hamsters! Pop your rival's bubbles in cute 1:1 turn-based battles.

**Full description** (max 4000)
A cute battle of wits between cats and hamsters inside bubbly bubbles! 🫧🐱🐹

Jump into a random match and face an anonymous player 1:1. It's quick to learn, but every move can turn the game around.

🎮 How to play
• Each side starts with 2 bubbles holding 1 critter each
• Add a bubble's critter count to an opponent bubble (attack) or your other bubble (boost)
• Reach 5 and 💥 POP! the bubble bursts
• A popped bubble comes back only with a ✂️ split (3 per game) or a 💖 revive potion
• Pop all your rival's bubbles to win!

🏆 Ranks and leaderboard
• Dirt → Copper → Silver → Gold → Diamond, with sparklier badges as you climb
• Higher ranks earn up to 20% bonus points on wins
• Climb the top 30 leaderboard

🛒 Shop
• 💖 Revive potion: bring a popped bubble back
• 🐑 Sheep · 🐶 Puppy · 🦒 Giraffe: change your character and cut your opponent's turn short for a match

🤖 AI battles
• Easy, Normal and Hard, with points for every win

🔒 Play with peace of mind
• Quick Google sign-in; opponents only see your nickname
• Scores and results are verified on our server, so they can't be faked

**Category**: Games > Board (or Puzzle)
